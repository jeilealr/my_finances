# Interactive Financial Dashboard

This module adds a local Streamlit dashboard to the existing `my_finances` repository.
It reads the normalized CSV outputs generated by the extractors and turns them into an
interactive analysis workspace for spending, balances, and monthly trends.

## Module Layout

- `app.py`: Streamlit entry point and page layout
- `cli.py`: launcher used by the package script and the `my_finances` CLI
- `data_loader.py`: CSV loading and path resolution
- `categories.py`: rule-based transaction categorization
- `analytics.py`: filtering, aggregation, net-worth, Payback debt, KPI, and report logic
- `charts.py`: Plotly figures used in the UI
- `investments.py`: PDF-derived ETF holdings, Alpha Vantage price cache, and Klarna
  interest helpers

## Categorization

The dashboard derives spending categories from the normalized transaction text. Categories
are rule-based and currently include:

- ATM Withdrawal
- Bank Fees
- Blocked Account
- Card Repayment
- Colombia Transfer
- Dance
- Electricity
- Fitness
- Grocery Shopping
- Income
- Interest Income
- Internal Transfer
- Investment Fees
- Investments
- Other
- Pharmacy
- Phone & Internet
- Rent
- Restaurants & Delivery
- Retail & Online Shopping
- Salary
- Savings
- Transport
- Travel
- Utilities

`Savings` represents principal movement into or out of savings accounts. It is treated as a
transfer, not as an expense. Interest paid by the Revolut Instant Access Savings account is
classified as `Interest Income`, so it remains visible as income.

`Investments`, `Blocked Account`, `Card Repayment`, and `Colombia Transfer` are
transfer-style extra transactions. Santander ETF purchases, Klarna blocked-account deposits,
Santander American Express repayments, Payback repayment rows, and Wise/TransferWise transfers
to Colombia are shown in chart notes, but they do not inflate
expense totals. The embedded `0.85 EUR` Santander ETF order fee is split out as
`Investment Fees`, so fees are counted as spending while ETF principal remains outside
spending.

## Dashboard Workflow

The sidebar uses one `Analysis period` calendar range to define the date window. Bank,
account, subaccount, and category filters are checkbox groups inside expanders. Each group
starts with `All` enabled; internally that means no filter is applied for that field.

The main tabs are:

- `Overview`: net-worth snapshots, bank-by-bank monthly cashflow, Payback debt tracking,
  and statement closing balances.
- `Spending`: one combined monthly expense view across Santander, Payback, and Revolut so
  transfers between your own accounts are not interpreted as separate spending.
- `Reports`: month-selected expense pie chart, grouped monthly expense trends, and excluded
  transfer context. A selected report group can also be broken down into its original
  categories month by month.
- `Budget`: UFZ salary, fixed recurring commitments, and money left after fixed costs.
- `Transactions`: the complete filtered transaction table.

## Budget

The Budget tab focuses on paycheck-based monthly limits. It uses only recurring
UFZ/Helmholtz paycheck rows with `Lohn/Gehalt`; travel reimbursements and one-off corrections
are excluded from the paycheck base.

Paycheck limits use fixed default percentages:

- `Housing & Utilities`: 32% of the paycheck.
- `Grocery`: 20% of the paycheck.
- `Other Spending Groups`: 10% of the paycheck, combining Entertainment, Travel & Holidays,
  Retail & Online Shopping, and Other.
- `Investment`: 18% of the paycheck, using Santander ETF principal only.
- `Remaining Target`: the unallocated paycheck target, normally 20%.

UFZ/Helmholtz salary is shifted one month forward for planning, because salary received in
month `n-1` funds budget month `n`. The tab also shows Spending-tab context for the same
month: total actual income, total expenses, and Spending money left.

## Spending

The Spending tab keeps charts expense-focused, but its `Monthly Expense Totals` table also
shows actual monthly income for comparison. Income is based on positive Santander/Revolut
rows classified as `Income`, including salary, reimbursements, refunds, tax refunds, and
Revolut savings interest. Payback credits and transfer-style movements are excluded. The
local `Focus categories` selector changes the expense totals but does not reduce the income
column.

## Reports

The Reports tab is expense-only. It groups filtered spending into higher-level report groups
that are separate from the raw sidebar categories:

- `Housing & Utilities`: Electricity, ATM Withdrawal, Bank Fees, Phone & Internet, Rent,
  Transport, and Pharmacy.
- `Grocery`: Grocery Shopping plus Restaurants & Delivery.
- `Entertainment`: Dance, Fitness, and clearly identifiable leisure/event merchants.
- `Travel & Holidays`: Travel plus any future Revolut Holidays-pocket expense rows.
- `Retail & Online Shopping`: retail and online purchases.
- `Other`: uncategorized merchant spending that remains after conservative merchant cleanup.

Salary, income, savings transfers, investments, card repayments, Colombia transfers, blocked
account movements, and internal transfers are excluded from the pie chart. Relevant excluded
movements are shown as context cards so they remain visible without inflating spending.

After the month-by-month group chart, the Reports tab includes a group selector that breaks
one group into its original categories. For example, `Housing & Utilities` can be split into
rent, electricity, transport, pharmacy, and other components by month.

## Net Worth, Investments, And Payback

The Overview tab includes a `Net Worth Snapshot`:

- `Total Net Worth`: Santander cash plus Revolut non-savings cash, Revolut savings, and
  Klarna blocked principal, minus known Payback debt.
- `Tracked Wealth`: Total Net Worth plus ETF market value and Klarna accrued interest.
- `Investment`: ETF market value when Alpha Vantage, Yahoo Finance, Boerse Frankfurt, or cached
  prices are available; otherwise PDF-derived cost basis. Holdings and cost basis come only
  from extracted Santander PDF rows.
- `ETF Gain/Loss`: ETF market value minus PDF-derived ETF cost basis.
- `Klarna Interest`: simple daily accrued interest on `6,000 EUR` at `2.79%` annually from
  `2026-03-11`; this is shown in wealth, not spending.
- `Blocked Account`: Klarna principal only.
- `Payback Known Debt`: known Payback purchases minus positive Payback credits/repayments,
  clipped at zero after each month when a repayment covers debt outside the loaded CSV
  history. If there are no newer Payback CSV rows, the last known debt is carried forward.

Payback is shown as a debt tracker instead of a normal income/expense account. Payback
purchases remain categorized expenses, while Payback repayment rows and Santander American
Express direct debits are treated as card-repayment transfers to avoid double-counting.

The currently filtered monthly report can be downloaded as CSV from the Reports tab.

## ETF Price Cache

ETF prices are optional runtime data. Set `ALPHA_VANTAGE_API_KEY` to let the dashboard refresh
daily prices from Alpha Vantage. Without the key, the dashboard tries no-key Yahoo Finance and
Boerse Frankfurt endpoints. Fetched prices are cached as CSV files under `outputs/market_prices/`,
so the dashboard can keep running when the API is unavailable.

If there is no API key and no cache, ETF valuation falls back to PDF-derived Santander cost
basis and the Overview tab shows a warning. If only a current quote is available, it is still
used for ETF market value so gain/loss remains visible. This fallback keeps `Total Net Worth`
and reports stable because investment principal is still treated as an extra transaction, not
spending.

## Run Locally

From the repository root:

```bash
conda activate finances
my_finances dashboard -- --server.headless true
```

Refresh all source statements before launching the dashboard with:

```bash
conda activate finances
my_finances dashboard --update
```

Streamlit arguments can still be forwarded after `--`:

```bash
my_finances dashboard --update -- --server.headless true
```

Or with the dedicated launcher:

```bash
conda activate finances
my_finances_dashboard --server.headless true
```

During normal local use you can omit `--server.headless true` to open it in the browser.

## Notes

- The current dashboard is EUR-only because the active workflow uses Santander, Payback,
  and Revolut.
- Revolut pocket balances are calculated from the running aggregate pocket balance in the
  statement, not by carrying old named-pocket balances forward.
- Blank subaccounts are normalized to `Main Account`.
- Internal transfers can be included or excluded from the non-report analytics from the
  sidebar. Reports always keep transfer data available so savings movement can be shown
  separately from spending.
